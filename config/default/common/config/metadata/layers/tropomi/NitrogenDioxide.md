### About Nitrogen Dioxide
Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2) is a reddish-brown toxic gas that is a key air quality indicator. NO2 is primarily emitted from combustion processes such as vehicle exhaust, power plants, and industrial facilities. High concentrations of NO2 in the atmosphere indicate poor air quality and can have significant health impacts. Prolonged exposure to NO2 can damage the respiratory system, particularly in children, the elderly, and individuals with asthma or other respiratory conditions. NO2 also plays a critical role in atmospheric chemistry, contributing to the formation of ground-level ozone and secondary organic aerosols, which further degrade air quality. Since NO2 concentrations vary significantly across different geographic regions and change rapidly throughout the day based on traffic patterns and industrial activity, satellite observations provide valuable near-real-time data for tracking pollution events and assessing regional air quality trends. NO2 data helps environmental agencies, researchers, and the public understand pollution patterns and take action to reduce emissions.
