The Net Photosynthesis (L4, 8-Day) layer is created from the NOAA-20 Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite (VIIRS) Gross Primary Productivity (GPP) and Net Photosynthesis (PSN) (VJ117A2) Version 2 data product. It is a cumulative composite of GPP values based on the radiation use efficiency concept that is potentially used as inputs to data models to calculate terrestrial energy, carbon, water cycle processes, and biogeochemistry of vegetation.

The imagery resolution is 500 m, and the temporal resolution is 8 days.
